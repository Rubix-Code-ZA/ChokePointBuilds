ChokePoint privacy policy

ChokePoint is a game by Rubix Code. This page says what it does with your data, which is: as little as possible.

What the game stores

Your progress (stars, best runs, achievements, which tutorials you have seen, your loadout choices and settings) is stored on your device only. Resetting progress in the game's settings erases it. Uninstalling the game erases it.

What the game sends

Nothing about you. The game has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no in-app purchases.

Test builds check for newer builds by reading a public GitHub releases page and, when you ask, download the new build from it. That is a plain web request that carries no identity.

If you sign in to Google Play Games from within the game, achievement unlocks are sent to Google Play Games Services, which is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. Signing in is optional and the game works fully without it.

When you share a run, the game hands your phone's share sheet a line of text and a link. The link contains the recorded run (the stage, the turrets you brought and your taps) and nothing else. Where it goes is your choice.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Contact
